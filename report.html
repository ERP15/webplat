Report / Presentation Guidelines

  • Structure: Title slide, Agenda, Problem, Approach, Demo, Results, Conclusion, References, Contact.
  • Design: Keep slides minimal — 3–6 bullet points per slide, large fonts, consistent headers, use our techy color palette.
  • Media: Embed the project video on the title or demo slide. For large videos, link to an external host (YouTube/Vimeo) or include a short preview.
  • Accessibility: High contrast, readable fonts, and descriptive alt text for images.
  • Export: To create a .pptx, copy slide content into PowerPoint or use tools that convert HTML to PPTX. Keep images and fonts local.
